# Privacy

Encore has no account, no telemetry and no update checks. Your library, listening history and
settings stay on your computer.

Encore reaches the network only for features you can turn off:

- **Online lyrics** from LrcLib, Musixmatch, Kugou and NetEase, asked with the title, artist,
  album and length of the song playing. Turn each source off in Settings → Lyrics.
- **Cover art for the Discord status**, looked up on Deezer by artist and album name while the
  status is on.
- **Discord Rich Presence**, which tells the Discord app running on your computer what plays.
- **Scrobbling** to the services you sign in to in Settings → Integrations.

Offline mode in Settings → Playback keeps Encore off the network altogether.
